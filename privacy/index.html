Privacy Policy

Soundtrail keeps your music library on your device by default. Optional online features have different data flows, explained below.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Soundtrail is an Android app, package tech.egrie.soundtrail, for saving music to places, creating playlists, and using optional location, motion, and time automations. It is maintained under the egrie-tech project by an operator based in Ontario, Canada. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Everett Grieve (egrie-tech), Ontario, Canada. Privacy contact: mob5824@proton.me.

This policy covers the app and the default Soundtrail sync/audio server we operate. The app is configured to connect to our server by default, and you may instead configure a self-hosted server. The default server is operated by the Soundtrail operator identified above. We self-host the default service in Canada. Its public URL has not yet been assigned; the endpoint will be identified in the app before use. Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a possible future hosting provider, not a current provider for our server. We will update this policy before a hosting change that materially affects how or where your information is handled. When you use an independently hosted Soundtrail server or a Jellyfin server, its operator controls its server-held information and its own policy also applies.

2. Information stored on your device

Soundtrail processes and stores the information you add: pin titles, artists, place labels, notes, music links, coordinates, creation times, playlist names and entries, and automation rules. Imported and downloaded audio is copied to private app storage. Connection settings and account labels are also stored to support the services you enable.

These library records stay on your device by default. Enabling cloud sync sends an encrypted version of supported library records to your selected server; exporting or sharing sends information to a destination you choose. Local audio and music-provider credentials are not part of cloud sync.

Soundtrail does not use an advertising ID or run advertising. The app does not include a separate general-purpose app-analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Google authentication-related usage information is discussed below. We do not sell personal information or use your library or Google user data for advertising. Separate music providers and server operators have their own practices, which are not controlled by this app.

3. Location, motion, and Android permissions

With your permission, Soundtrail uses approximate or precise device location to place pins and evaluate place-based automations. Its optional automation watch runs as a foreground location service with an ongoing notification and may continue when the app is not on screen. Motion categories such as walking, running, or driving are estimated on-device from GPS speed; the app does not use an Activity Recognition API for this.

Saved pin coordinates and automation locations are stored locally and included in encrypted cloud sync if you enable it. Map requests can reveal the area you view, as described below. The app does not send a continuous raw GPS movement history to the sync service as part of its documented sync protocol.

You can turn off the automation watch or revoke location permission in Android settings. Some features will then be unavailable. Notification permission supports automation alerts and playback controls. Internet access supports online features. The optional Google connection can use Android account permission to find Google-type accounts on your device. Limited installed-app checks detect supported music apps and Google/microG services for handoff and connection setup; they are not a general inventory of installed apps.

4. Optional music-service connections

Only connect a service if you want its features. Requests to a provider expose your IP address and ordinary connection metadata, in addition to the information described here. Providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Spotify: OAuth with PKCE authorizes track search. Search text and authenticated API requests go to Spotify. Access and refresh credentials are stored encrypted on-device using Android Keystore. Opening a track hands its link to Spotify or a browser.
  • SoundCloud: OAuth with PKCE authorizes search. SoundCloud receives search text and authentication requests. Tokens and a configured client secret are stored encrypted on-device. Playback links open in an external app or browser.
  • Jellyfin: Your chosen server receives sign-in credentials, music-library searches, playback, and authorized download requests. Soundtrail does not store the Jellyfin password; its access token is stored encrypted on-device. Library records use item references rather than embedding the server address or token in backups or sync.
  • TheAudioDB: Artist and song-title lookup sends that text to TheAudioDB for music metadata. It requires no user account. Results can be used to fill a pin.
  • YouTube Music handoff: Search or library actions open the installed music app or website. Search text or song links go to that app or website. Soundtrail does not read the music app’s private storage or session.

Google and YouTube API data

If you explicitly connect Google, Soundtrail requests your email and the YouTube youtube.readonly scope. It stores the selected account name, type, and display label encrypted on-device, and retrieves metadata for videos in your own YouTube playlists through the official YouTube Data API. This does not provide access to the entire YouTube Music library. Playlist metadata is temporarily cached in memory; search matching happens on-device, rather than sending the playlist-search text to YouTube. Entries you choose to save become local library records and may be included in backups or encrypted sync.

The Google OAuth access token is requested from Google services or compatible microG when needed, used in memory for API calls, and is not persisted by Soundtrail or sent to its sync/audio server. Google/microG may independently manage account credentials.

Google authentication analytics: Google’s authentication services may process sign-in activity, service-usage information, and diagnostic metadata under the Google Privacy Policy. Authentication-related usage information made available to us is used to monitor the Google connection, not to profile your movements or music listening. We do not send pin coordinates, notes, or audio files to Google for analytics. This is separate from the playlist metadata you authorize Soundtrail to retrieve.

Google user data is used only to provide the playlist features you request, not for advertising, sale, or general-purpose AI training. It is not made available to people for routine inspection. Transfers of saved entries occur through your requested exports or encrypted sync. Soundtrail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting the YouTube connection clears Soundtrail’s stored account references and memory cache and asks Google services to revoke access on a best-effort basis. You can also revoke access through your Google account’s third-party connections. Disconnecting a provider does not automatically delete songs you previously saved or data the provider retains.

5. Map tiles and network metadata

The map loads OpenStreetMap-based raster tiles from CARTO’s public tile service at basemaps.cartocdn.com. CARTO receives your IP address, tile coordinates, zoom level, request timing, and app User-Agent. Tile coordinates reveal the visible map area, which can include your current or saved location. Individual pin records, notes, and exact pin coordinates are rendered locally and are not added to tile requests. Tiles are cached in memory.

Although we do not retain server access/request logs on our default service, third-party music services, Google authentication services, network providers, and independent server operators may record IP addresses and request metadata. Their logging, retention, and processing locations depend on their own practices. Soundtrail’s local-first design does not mean that online features make no network disclosures.

6. Default server and self-hosted encrypted sync

Our server is the default connection endpoint; you can choose a self-hosted endpoint instead. A connection to a server exposes your IP address and connection timing even before library content is uploaded. If you register or sign in, the selected server processes your email address and password for account creation and login. The supplied server implementation stores salted password hashes, hashed session tokens, expiry information, encrypted sync records, event IDs and sequences, and related account/session metadata. Its host or reverse proxy may log IP addresses and request timing.

Pins (including coordinates and notes), playlist metadata, music links, and automation rules are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before upload. The server stores encrypted events and checkpoints and can see their sizes and timing, but does not receive the plaintext library content or recovery key through this protocol. Local audio files and music-provider credentials are excluded.

You control your recovery key. Losing it can make encrypted information unrecoverable; changing the account password cannot recover it. End-to-end encryption of library content does not hide your account email, IP address, or traffic metadata. Your chosen server operator’s privacy and security practices remain important.

7. Optional YouTube audio extraction

This feature is separate from the official read-only YouTube API and is not end-to-end encrypted. After you confirm you own the content or have permission to download it, Soundtrail sends the video ID and the request under your authenticated Soundtrail server account. The server contacts YouTube and temporarily processes the audio before transferring it to your device.

The supplied server does not use Google OAuth tokens, YouTube login cookies, or a YouTube Music session for extraction. Temporary audio files are removed when the response finishes or fails in normal operation. Crashes, host backups, and server logs may require separate operator cleanup and retention rules. The resulting audio stays in private app storage and is not cloud-synced.

8. Exports, imported files, and sharing

A JSON export contains pins, coordinates, notes, playlists, song links, and automation rules. It is a readable, unencrypted file, unlike encrypted sync. Login credentials and audio bytes are excluded. You choose where to save it; a file-storage provider you choose may receive it. Protect exported files and delete copies you no longer need.

Imports read the file you select and merge supported records into your library. Audio import copies the selected audio into app storage. Playback or link sharing can grant the player or app you choose access to the selected audio or link. Soundtrail does not control what the receiving app does with that information.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: Library records remain until you delete them or clear app data/uninstall. You can remove pins, playlist entries, and automations, and manage individual offline audio files or clear all offline copies. Removing a song or playlist removes its attached app-managed audio copy, not your original source file.
  • Provider connections: Disconnect within Soundtrail to remove local connection credentials. Revoke grants or manage provider-held data with the provider separately.
  • Sync: Disconnecting sync does not delete the remote account. Use the cloud-sync account deletion action to delete the account, sessions, encrypted events, receipts, and checkpoint from the supplied server’s active database. This leaves the device library untouched. Deletions of individual records propagate when successfully synced; encrypted deletion markers and history may remain until compaction or account deletion.
  • Other copies: Exported backups, libraries on other devices, provider records, and server backup/log copies are not automatically erased by deleting an app record or remote account. Manage those separately.

We do not retain server access/request logs on our self-hosted default service. This does not mean that the server stores no data: account, session, and encrypted sync records described above remain while needed to provide your account, until expiry, compaction, or account deletion as applicable. Google authentication-related information is handled separately as described in section 4; Google controls retention of its own records. The supplied server software does not automatically create database backups. If we introduce a managed backup process, we will disclose its retention and deletion rules. If you cannot sign in to delete an account, email mob5824@proton.me; we will help after proportionate identity verification. We may retain limited information where required by law and will explain any applicable exception to a deletion request. If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your request, provide support, or resolve a privacy concern. We retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose and any applicable legal obligations. For independently hosted servers, ask that operator about retention and deletion.

10. Security and processing locations

Soundtrail uses app-private storage, Android Keystore-backed encryption for stored connection secrets, and on-device encryption for sync payloads. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the reviewed build. Not all local library records or exported backups are separately encrypted. No system can guarantee complete security.

Use HTTPS for sync and provider connections. Jellyfin can be configured over plain HTTP on a trusted local network; HTTP can expose credentials and traffic to others on that network. Secure your device, recovery key, exports, and self-hosted servers.

Online providers or your selected host may process information in countries other than yours, where legal protections differ. On-device processing takes place on your device. The processing location for optional sync or audio extraction depends on the server you select. Consult that operator and each music provider about their locations and safeguards before connecting. Our location in Ontario does not mean all provider or server processing takes place in Canada.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use local features without enabling cloud sync or connecting every provider, control Android permissions, stop the automation watch, export your library, and use the deletion choices above. These choices may limit particular features.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a portable copy of, object to, or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a privacy regulator. Contact mob5824@proton.me for operator-held information. We may need proportionate verification of your identity. We cannot retrieve data stored only on your device or decrypt sync content without the user-held key; please do not send us that key.

For information under our control, we will handle access and correction requests, consent withdrawals, and privacy complaints in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies. Withdrawal of consent does not undo processing already lawfully performed and may prevent us from providing a requested feature. Where applicable, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Other mandatory rights may apply where you live. Send requests about independent servers or music-provider records to their operators.

12. Children and age requirements

Soundtrail’s services are intended for adults aged 18 or older who have reached the age of majority where they live; they are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through services we operate. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact the privacy contact below so we can investigate and delete information under our control where appropriate. Local device records and independently operated server accounts must be managed on that device or with that server operator. This policy statement is not a claim that the app technically verifies age.
