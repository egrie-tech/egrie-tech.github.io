2. What Soundtrail provides

Soundtrail lets you save private location-based music pins, organize local playlists, import audio, export/import library records, configure opt-in place, motion, and time automations, build on-device mood mixes, ask for music by voice or text, and browse the library through Android Auto. Optional integrations support provider searches, in-app playback (Jellyfin streams, your own files, Spotify previews, SoundCloud streams, YouTube audio prepared by your own server, and attached offline copies) with device media controls, handoff to an installed music app, encrypted sync with our default server or a self-hosted server you choose, permission-confirmed YouTube audio extraction, and optional AI mood readings through a provider you select.

Features depend on your device, permissions, connectivity, provider authorization, model availability, and server configuration. Soundtrail does not supply ownership of music, a subscription to any provider, an AI model, or a speech engine. Automations produce prompts; location and speed estimates can be inaccurate or unavailable. Voice recognition can mishear a request. AI readings and model-suggested songs can be wrong, incomplete, or fabricated. This is not a navigation, emergency, or safety-critical service.

3. Accounts, credentials, and recovery keys

A fresh install falls back to our server address at https://soundtrail.duckdns.org if you do not enter another one, and you may choose a self-hosted server instead. A Soundtrail account is required for server features such as sync and the authenticated audio extractor, not for local library features; the server is contacted only when you use them. If you use sync, use accurate account information and keep your password, server session, and encryption recovery key secure. Only connect accounts and servers you are authorized to access, and tell the relevant operator if you suspect unauthorized access.

The sync recovery key is needed to decrypt your library. The supplied server cannot reset or reconstruct it, and changing an account password does not recover encrypted data. The reviewed server implementation has no email verification, no password-reset service, and no key-recovery endpoint; deleting the account is a self-service action. Sessions expire after 30 days, and encrypted sync data for each account is capped at 64 MiB. Keep a secure backup of your key and library; do not share credentials or the recovery key in public support requests.

API keys and tokens for music providers, Google, Ollama, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or Google AI Studio are your own credentials. Keep them secure, use a key with only the access you intend to grant, and comply with that provider’s terms and any usage charges. Soundtrail stores these secrets encrypted with Android Keystore where the app is the holder, excludes them from sync and exports, and removes them when you disconnect or press Remove. A key pasted into a self-hosted or third-party endpoint is handled by that operator as well.

If you run a server, you are responsible for its deployment, updates, HTTPS, access controls, storage, logs, backups, and any privacy or legal obligations to its users. Hosting Soundtrail code does not make the project maintainer the operator of your server. The self-host guide covers the supported setup, including the loopback-only default, the reverse proxy, and backing up the database folder, which is the only way to recover an encrypted event log.

5. Music ownership and download permissions

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions needed to import, download, store, play, or share any audio. Access to a stream, a public link, a model suggestion, or a Jellyfin library does not necessarily grant permission to download or redistribute it.

The optional YouTube extractor is unofficial and separate from the official YouTube Data API. Use it only for content you own or have explicit permission to download, and only where that use complies with applicable law and provider terms. Soundtrail requires a rights confirmation before the request, but that confirmation does not itself create permission or make a download lawful. Extraction requests are rate-limited and each resulting track is capped at 250 MiB, as are Jellyfin downloads.

The supplied extractor uses the third-party yt-dlp project, accepts one YouTube video ID per request, and does not use login cookies; it is not intended to access private, age-restricted, login-gated, DRM-protected, or otherwise restricted content. Jellyfin downloads are subject to the account permissions enforced by your server. Do not use either feature to evade restrictions. Providers may change or block access at any time.

The same rules apply to a song found through Ask, a mood mix, or an imported playlist: finding a link does not make it yours to download. A model’s suggestion is not a licence, and the app verifies the song exists rather than granting rights.
